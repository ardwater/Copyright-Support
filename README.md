Refunds
Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Location not working
Cereal Ride needs location access to measure your ride. Go to iPhone Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Location Services, then Cereal Ride, and choose While Using the App.

Elevation not showing
Height climbed uses your iPhone's barometer through Motion & Fitness. Make sure Motion & Fitness is allowed for Cereal Ride in iPhone Settings.

Deleting your data
Your ride history only exists on your iPhone. Delete a single ride from its page, delete everything with Delete all history in the History tab, or delete the app.
